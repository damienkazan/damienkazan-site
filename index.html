About

Hi! I'm Damien Kazan. I've always had a strong passion for movies and I've been directing short films for 15 years. Some of my shorts have been selected in small film festivals (not Cannes nor Sundance, I'm not that fancy but still very very cool nonetheless and happy about each selection), I've won a Best Film award once (Rouen Fantastic Film Festival) but most of my shorts have dropped directly to Youtube because festival circuit is rough and expensive (no offense to festivals out there, it is the reality of 99% of indie filmmakers).

Short films are difficult to make and they rarely find a big audience, but I can't help wanting to make them. There's a 100 stuff I'd like to film.

I've recently done a fan film for the first time and if you ask me I'd like to do 2 more, a super small Batman one for fun, a Red Dead Redemption one because I love westerns! (Also a Red Dead Undead one would be really cool) and actually now that I think of it, a Star Wars one as well. But these would require too much budget so not sure if they'll ever see the light of day.

I'd also love to do an original animated short film.

All my work is 100% human made, crafted by hand, NO generative AI.

If you'd like to collaborate, need me for something or just say hi, feel free to contact me below! :)
