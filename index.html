About

Hi! I'm Damien Kazan. I've always had a strong passion for movies and I've been directing short films for 15 years. Some of my shorts have been selected in small film festivals (not Cannes nor Sundance, I'm not that fancy but still very very cool nonetheless and happy about each selection), I've won a main prize once (Rouen Fantastic Film Festival) but most of my shorts have dropped directly to Youtube because festival circuit is rough and expensive (no offense to festivals out there, it is the truth of 99% of indie filmmakers).

Short films are difficult to make and they rarely find a big audience, but I can't help wanting to make them. There's a 100 stuff I'd like to film.

I've recently done a fan film for the first time and if you ask me I'd like to do 2 more, a small short Batman one and Red Dead Redemption because I love westerns! (Also a Red Dead Undead one would be really cool). Actually a Star Wars one too but that requires too much budget.

I'd also love to do an original animated short film.

All my work is 100% human made, crafted by hand, NO generative AI. I don't wanna use Gen AI in my films because what's the point? I'm interested in crafting films and see them come to life through the process of filming and editing, not typing prompts. I see no value in generated shots.

If you'd like to collaborate, need me for something or just say hi, feel free to contact me below! :)
